Cortado e Servido Uploader for TikTok

Uploads and publishes the short podcast clips of the @cortadoeservido channel to its own TikTok account.

Privacy PolicyTerms of Service

What it is

Private, single-user tool of the TikTok account @cortadoeservido. The account owner edits short clips (podcast cuts) on their own computer, and this tool uploads them to the owner's own TikTok account with the TikTok Content Posting API, with title, caption and privacy setting chosen by the owner.

It is not offered to anyone else: there is no sign-up and no access to data from other TikTok users.

Posting a clip, step by step

  1. Sign in with TikTok. The owner opens the app and is sent to TikTok's login page (Login Kit). After authorizing, TikTok returns to the app with an access token for the owner's account.
  2. Check the account. The posting screen shows the display name and avatar of the connected account, so the owner sees where the video will go.
  3. Preview and caption. The screen plays the clip and shows its caption, which can be edited before posting.
  4. Choose privacy and interactions. The owner selects the privacy level from the options TikTok returns for the account, and decides whether comments, Duet and Stitch are allowed.
  5. Publish. The owner presses the publish button. The app uploads the video and shows the status reported by TikTok.

video.uploadContent Posting APISend a clip to the owner's TikTok drafts, to be finished in the TikTok app.

Content

The videos are clips of Brazilian podcasts, up to 3 minutes long, published according to the original creators' rules for clips. Every post credits the original show and points to the full episode.

Data

The access token stays on the owner's computer and is used only to post to the owner's account. The app does not collect or share data from any other TikTok user. Access can be revoked at any time in the TikTok app (Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions). Details in the Privacy Policy.
