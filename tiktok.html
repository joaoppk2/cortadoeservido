Cortado e Servido Uploader for TikTok

Private, single-user tool of the TikTok account @cortadoeservido. The account owner edits short clips (podcast cuts) on their own computer, and this tool uploads them to the owner's own TikTok account with the TikTok Content Posting API, with title, caption and privacy setting chosen by the owner.

It is not offered to anyone else: there is no sign-up and no access to data from other TikTok users.
