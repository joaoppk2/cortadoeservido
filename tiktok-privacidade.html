Privacy Policy — Cortado e Servido Uploader for TikTok

Last updated: October 3, 2026.

This tool is used only by the owner of the TikTok account @cortadoeservido to publish their own videos on that account through the TikTok Login Kit and Content Posting API.

Data we use

  • The owner's own TikTok access token (scopes user.info.basic, video.upload, video.publish), used only to upload and publish videos to the owner's account.
  • Basic profile information of the owner's account (display name and avatar), shown only to confirm the right account is connected.
  • Videos, titles and captions created by the owner.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect, store or share data from any other TikTok user.
  • We do not sell data, show ads or use cookies.

Storage and deletion

The access token is stored only on the owner's computer. Access can be revoked at any time in the TikTok app (Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions), which ends all access by this tool.
